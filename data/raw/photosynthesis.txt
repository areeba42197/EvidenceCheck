Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae and some bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy. In plants it takes place in chloroplasts.
Using light, carbon dioxide and water, photosynthesis produces glucose and releases oxygen.
Chlorophyll is the main pigment involved. It absorbs mostly blue and red light and reflects green light, which is why leaves look green.
The light-dependent reactions occur in the thylakoid membranes. The Calvin cycle, which fixes carbon dioxide into sugars, occurs in the stroma.
