EvidenceCheck is a research prototype that evaluates whether claims in language model answers are supported by retrieved evidence.
Documents are split into overlapping chunks of characters. The default chunk size is 500 characters with an overlap of 100 characters.
Chunks are embedded locally with a sentence transformer model and stored in a FAISS vector index.
At query time the retriever returns the top-k chunks ranked by cosine similarity. The default value of k is 3.
The generated answer is split into atomic claims. Each claim is compared with its retrieved evidence and labelled supported, partially supported, or unsupported.
The reliability score is the number of supported claims divided by the number of verifiable claims.
